Overland from Singapore to Bangkok

By train the whole way, arriving in Bangkok on New Year's Eve.

The Malaysia→Thailand run takes two days, so the middle of the trip is shaped around catching one sleeper train that leaves once a day at 17:00. Singapore gets two full days; Kuala Lumpur gets one. Everything before 31 December exists to put us in Bangkok in time for the fireworks.

The one real fork in the trip — how we cover Kuala Lumpur to Bangkok on 30 December.

A · Overnight sleeper, train no. 46

16h 05m, departs 17:00, arrives 11:00. Cheapest, and the berth doubles as that night's bed — but we land the morning of New Year's Eve with zero slack, and it runs once a day.

What this layout does to our content

  • The add-ons section is a straight win. Universal, the Oceanarium, the Luge and the eSIM really are products with a price each and a price ×5. Cards beat the table.
  • The option tiles suit the sleeper-vs-fly fork. Two priced choices side by side, one marked recommended, is clearer than the current prose comparison.
  • A sticky action panel gives the two urgent bookings somewhere permanent to live. Right now they're buried in a status table.
  • The headline price can't be honest. The layout's most prominent element wants one number; we can't produce one until flights and hotels are booked. What's shown above is a floor with three exclusions written under it — which is exactly the kind of hedging this layout is designed to hide.
  • It is built for a decided product; ours is half-undecided. Every "not booked" chip is friction the marketplace layout treats as an error state. Our current page treats undecided as first-class information, which is the thing that actually helps us.
  • It needs photography we don't have. Nine image slots on this page, zero images in the workspace. Without them the mosaic and the cards read as broken rather than minimal.
  • The itinerary loses information when it collapses. Day 3 now carries the full Universal Studios ride order, and it fits — but only once you open it. Ten of eleven days stay shut, the summary line can hold about six words, and the side blocks (tickets, transport, watch-outs) had to be flattened into a footnote. Our day cards show all of that at once, with the country rails carrying the colour.
