Before we fly

Three countries, three digital arrival cards. All mandatory
  • Singapore Arrival Card: a free online form, submitted within 72 hours before landing. Airlines check it before boarding, and you show the QR code at immigration.
  • Malaysia Digital Arrival Card: the same idea, within 3 days before arrival. Required since January 2024.
  • Thailand Digital Arrival Card: up to 72 hours before arrival. Required for foreign visitors since May 2025.
  • These are per person and per country: fifteen submissions across the five of us, each inside its own 3-day window. They can't all be done before we fly. Put reminders in everyone's phone now.
  • All three are free on the official government sites. Don't pay a third-party site to file them.

Everyone, individually

  • Passport valid at least 6 months past 25 Dec 2026
  • Proof of onward travel, both legs: the SG→JB train booking (leaves Singapore) and the return flight (leaves Thailand). See the flag up top
  • Singapore Arrival Card submitted (within 72 hours of the 26 Dec landing)
  • Malaysia Arrival Card submitted (within 3 days of the Malaysia crossing)
  • Thailand Arrival Card submitted (within 72 hours of entering Thailand)
  • Travel insurance
  • eSIM bought and installed. Check your phone supports eSIM first
  • Transit card plan: EZ-Link/SimplyGo · Touch 'n Go · Rabbit
  • Cards told you're travelling; a backup card kept separately
  • Offline maps downloaded for all three cities
