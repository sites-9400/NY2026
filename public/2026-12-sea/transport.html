Singapore trains (MRT)

Six lines, each with a colour and a two-letter code. Every station has a code like DT12: the line, then the stop. Trains run about 5:30am to midnight, every few minutes.

Kuala Lumpur trains

Several lines built by different operators, joined up later. KL Sentral is the hub. Some changes mean a 5 to 10 minute walk between stations, so allow extra time.

KL is hard to walk at street level. The exception: a covered, air-conditioned walkway runs from Pavilion, Bukit Bintang to KLCC, about 15 to 20 minutes.

New Year's Eve: getting home
  • Rapid KL runs trains late on New Year's Eve: until 3am for New Year 2025, until 2am for New Year 2026. This year's hours come out around 26 Dec. Check MyRapid PULSE.
  • KLCC station is crushed after midnight and can be closed or held. Walk to Bukit Bintang or Ampang Park instead.
  • Grab surge pricing after midnight is steep and cars are scarce. Don't count on it.
  • Agree a meeting point before midnight. Phones may not get signal in the crowd.
