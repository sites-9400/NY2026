SG 3KL 2BKK 3Pattaya 1NYE · Bangkok

By land the whole way from Singapore to Bangkok: a night on the sleeper train, then a Pattaya beach finish. Cheapest, and the one adventure route.

DAY1Davao → Cebu → SingaporeFLYFri 25›

Christmas Day in the air.

  • DVO 13:00 → CEB, 6h 30m layover (dinner outside the airport), CEB 20:40 → land Changi 00:25 on the 26th
  • No MRT this late: Grab into town, ~01:30 check-in. Five + luggage is two cars
Watch out
  • SG hotel booked for the night of Fri 25, arrival flagged so the room isn't released
DAY2Singapore · half day, slow startSat 26›

Up around noon, no alarms.

  • Jewel Changi (Rain Vortex) while we're rested, we arrived in the dark
  • Gardens by the Bay (Flower Dome + Cloud Forest), Garden Rhapsody
  • Marina Bay + Merlion, supper at Lau Pa Sat satay street
DAY3Universal Studios · SentosaSun 27›

Be at the gate for opening.

  • Rope-drop Transformers + Battlestar Galactica, then Revenge of the Mummy, Minion Land, Jurassic Park Rapids, WaterWorld
  • Dated Klook tickets, peak week. Pack tonight, tomorrow's a travel day
DAY4Singapore → Johor Bahru → KL · overlandMon 28›

A travel day. Be at Woodlands by midday.

  • Shuttle Tebrau to Johor Bahru (5 min ride, 20–40 with checkpoints, S$5)
  • ETS JB → KL Sentral, ~4h 20m, RM73–81. Check in the evening
Watch out
  • Malaysia Arrival Card due within 3 days of this crossing
DAY5Kuala Lumpur · the one full dayTue 29›
  • Batu Caves early, before the heat
  • Merdeka Square cluster (walkable), Thean Hou Temple
  • Petronas Towers at dusk, Jalan Alor night food street
Watch out
  • Confirm tomorrow's train tickets tonight, everything depends on them
DAY6KL → the Thai border → BangkokWed 30›

The tightest link in the trip.

  • ETS KL → Padang Besar, ~5h 30m. Take the early 07:08, ~5h of slack at the border
  • Sleeper #46 departs 17:00 Thai (18:00 Malaysian), the only one a day. The berth is the bed
  • Or fly the last hop: cross to Hat Yai, fly to Bangkok (~₱2,000, 15 flights/day) and sleep there a day early option
DAY7Arrive Bangkok · New Year's EveNYEThu 31›
  • Sleeper arrives Krung Thep Aphiwat ~09:05. Check in, shower, sleep it off
  • Evening: NYE fireworks (riverside / ICONSIAM, spot not yet chosen)
Watch out
  • We arrive the same day, no slack. Transport gets ugly late: walk or BTS
DAY8Bangkok · Siam shoppingFri 1›

Low-effort, indoor, doubles as the recovery day.

  • Siam by BTS: MBK, Siam Paragon, CentralWorld, Erawan Shrine, all by skywalk
  • Optional Platinum Fashion Mall at Pratunam. Keep receipts for the VAT refund
DAY9Bangkok · old town + the riverSat 2›
  • Wat Pho, Grand Palace, cross to Wat Arun
  • Chao Phraya Express boat, ICONSIAM or a dinner cruise in the evening
Watch out
  • Grand Palace dress code (shoulders/knees). Socks for temples
DAY10Bangkok → Pattaya · to the coastSun 3›
  • Bus or van to Pattaya (~1.5–2.5h from Ekkamai, ~฿148)
  • Sanctuary of Truth, beach (Jomtien is calmer), sunset viewpoint. Overnight Pattaya
DAY11Pattaya → Davao · fly homeFLYMon 4›
  • Relaxed morning (Koh Larn if back by early afternoon)
  • Pattaya → Don Mueang directly (~2–2.5h). 21:15 CebPac DMK → Davao, direct, lands ~02:00
SG 3KL 2BKK 4NYE · Bangkok

Fly the hops. Keeps NYE in Bangkok and wins back Singapore's culture day. KL is the short one at two days.

Getting around: fly SIN→KL (~₱2–3.5k) and KL→BKK (NYE-peak ~₱6–10k on 31 Dec). Home: Mon 4 Jan, CebPac DMK→DVO.
