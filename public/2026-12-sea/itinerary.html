Bus options · 3
  • Aeroline 18:00. Hot meal and toilet on board. Boards in Jurong (1 Venture Ave), ~30 min by Grab, so leave 17:15. Drops at Corus Hotel, next to KLCC. ~S$50, ~₱2,400 each. Book at aeroline.com.sg
  • StarMart 19:00. Golden Mile Tower, Beach Road, ~10 min by Grab → Berjaya Times Square, Bukit Bintang, ~01:00. Rated 4.2 (95 reviews). S$31.50, ~₱1,500 each. No meal
  • CityLine 19:05. Lavender MRT → Bukit Bintang, 00:56. S$38, ~₱1,800 each. Rated 3.6. No meal
  • Fallback, the train: Shuttle Tebrau 13:45 + ETS 15:00, KL Sentral 19:20, ~₱1,350–1,760 each
Watch out · 4
