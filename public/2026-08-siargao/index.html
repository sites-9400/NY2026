Three things that can bite, and all three are cheap to deal with now
  • The tickets cannot be changed. They were bought through an agency, and the ticket policy on that booking says the tickets may not be changed and cannot be cancelled for a refund. The airline's own fare rules are looser, so if a date ever has to move, that is a conversation with the agency first, not the airline. Treat 29 Aug and 1 Sep as fixed.
  • Late August is wet season. Rain is normal and passes; a typhoon is not, and it cancels flights. Because the tickets cannot be changed, a storm is the one thing that could cost the whole trip. Watch the forecast from about a week out.
  • No checked bag on any of the four tickets. Hand carry only, 7 kg each, one piece no larger than 56 × 36 × 23 cm. Buying an allowance at the airport counter costs more than adding it online. Four days on a beach probably fits. Decide before we pack.
One date to keep: 14 August
  • The room is free to cancel until 23:59 on 14 August, island time. After that it drops to a 66% refund, and from 29 August it is worth nothing.
  • So the last honest moment to change our minds about the whole trip is 14 August. The flights are already unchangeable, but the room is the bigger number.

Quieter corners

We have done General Luna, Cloud 9 and the three-island run more than once. This is the shortlist of what we have not, weighted towards empty beaches and things that are not a boat full of other people. Nothing here is booked, and no price is checked unless it says so.

What the free bike actually changes
  • The north stops being a van hire and starts being a tank of fuel. Alegria and Pacifico were always the answer to "somewhere quiet", and the reason people skip them is the cost and hassle of getting there. That reason is gone.
  • One direction per day. North is a three-hour round trip before we stop anywhere. Trying to pair it with Del Carmen turns a holiday into a commute.
  • Fuel up in General Luna. North of Pacifico it is bottles at the sari-sari store.
  • Do not ride back after dark if we can avoid it. No lights, dogs, and potholes that do not show up until you are in them.

Distances and the two prices marked above come from a check on 29 July 2026. Everything else is a suggestion, not a booking, and nothing has been confirmed with an operator.

What GO Basic actually includes
  • Hand carry only, 7 kg each. No checked bag on any of the four tickets.
  • Seats unassigned. Assigned at check-in, so check in early to sit together.
  • No changes and no refunds. The airline's own GO Basic rules allow rebooking for a fee, but the agency these were bought through says the tickets may not be changed or cancelled at all. Assume the stricter of the two.
  • Counters and bag drop close 1 hour before departure. Be at the gate 45 minutes before.

  • Until 23:59 on 14 August, island time: free cancellation, everything back.

Still to ask them
  • The bike. Which one, whether there are two helmets, and whether it goes back with a full tank. Worth knowing before we plan a three-hour ride north on it.
  • Airport transfer. If Coracasa arranges it at the ₱300 we usually pay, that settles both the Saturday arrival and the early Tuesday start in one message.
  • Breakfast on Tuesday. We leave around 07:45. Ask whether they can do it early, since it is already paid for.
  • Arrival time. Tell them we land at 13:05 and reach town around 14:30.
  • Power. Outages happen on the island. Worth knowing if there is a generator.

Bring cash, more than feels sensible
  • ATMs on Siargao are few, and they run out. The reliable move is to draw what we need in Davao before we fly.
  • Boats, habal-habal, island fees and most small places are cash only.
  • Small notes are worth having. Change for a thousand is not always available.

Before we fly

Domestic flight, so no passport and no arrival card. The list is short.

  • Valid photo ID for each of us, with the name matching the booking exactly
  • Check in online as soon as it opens, so we get seats together
  • Weigh the bags. 7 kg hand carry each, and nothing checked
  • Buy a baggage allowance online if we decide we need one. Cheaper than at the counter
  • Draw cash in Davao. See the note above
  • Message Coracasa: arrival time, the bike and helmets, airport transfer, early breakfast on Tuesday
  • Driving licences on us, not left at home
  • Book the Tuesday morning transfer the night before
  • Book the anniversary dinner
  • Check the tide table for Monday if we go north to Magpupungko
  • Reef-safe sunscreen, rash guards, a dry bag
  • Offline maps for the island
  • Watch the weather in the week before. Late August is wet season
Two things to confirm locally
  • Environmental fee. Siargao collects one from arriving visitors. Have small notes ready at Sayak. The amount is not confirmed here, so do not budget off a guess.
  • The bike. Licence on us, helmets on, and check the brakes before the first long ride rather than halfway to Alegria.
