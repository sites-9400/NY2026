## Quieter corners, checked 29 Jul 2026

Researched because Eve and Jude have done General Luna, Cloud 9 and the three-island run
already and wanted somewhere emptier.

- **Alegria** — northern tip, roughly **1½ hours by bike** from General Luna. White sand,
  palms, very few people. Three hours of riding round trip, so it is a whole day, and there is
  little food up there.
- **Pacifico** — north-east coast, on the way to Alegria. Sleepy surf village under the palms,
  long empty beach, quiet break. Bring cash, few options.
- **A hidden beach off a dirt track near Pacifico** — described in more than one place but with
  no exact location. On the page marked unverified. Ask locally.
- **Del Carmen mangrove boardwalk** — **free**. Largest mangrove forest in the country.
- **Firefly watching, Del Carmen river** — after dark, by boat or paddleboard, sometimes sold
  bundled with a sunset paddle. Not priced: the one operator listing found was a foreign-
  currency package and not a fair local number.
- **Taktak Falls**, Santa Monica — **₱50 each plus ₱20 parking**. Road goes nearly to the base,
  so a few minutes' walk. About 15 to 20 minutes from the Del Carmen sights.
- **Sohoton Cove**, Bucas Grande, by boat from Del Carmen — **₱1,500 to ₱2,500 each**, full
  day. **Jellyfish sanctuary is seasonal, best roughly November to May**, so late August misses
  it. Caves and lagoons are year round.
- **Kawhagan** — named as an add-on to the standard island-hopping run. Not researched further.
- **Pasikon Beach** — came up as another quiet beach. Not looked into. Worth a search.

Sources: siargao.ph, journeyera.com, thecoastalcampaign.com, maridenresort.com guides.
Prices are what those pages state, not what we have paid, so treat them as indicative.

## Places, unverified and unpriced

**General Luna** is the town where almost everything is. About an hour by road from
Sayak Airport, which is in Del Carmen on the other side of the island.

- **Cloud 9** — boardwalk out over the reef, the surf break the island is known for.
  Faces east, so it is a sunrise spot rather than a sunset one.
- **Island hopping** — Naked (a bare sandbar, no shade at all), Daku (the big one, where
  lunch usually happens), Guyam (small, a few palms). Boats go from General Luna in the
  morning and are back mid-afternoon.
- **Sugba Lagoon** — Del Carmen side. Drive to the port, then a boat out. Registration
  and boat hire happen at the port, and boats stop going out in the afternoon.
- **Magpupungko rock pools** — north, near Pilar. **Low tide only.** At high tide the
  pools are under the sea.
- **Pacifico, Alegria, Maasin River, the coconut road viewpoint** — the rest of the
  north loop, all in the same direction as Magpupungko.
- **Sohoton Cove**, on Bucas Grande, is a full day by boat and probably does not fit in
  a trip this short.
