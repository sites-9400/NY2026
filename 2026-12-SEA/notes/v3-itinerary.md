# Version 3 — the equal 3/3/3 · NYE in Kuala Lumpur

Chosen 2026-08-15. Full day-by-day draft. Singapore, Kuala Lumpur and Bangkok get
**three nights each**, all hops flown, **no Pattaya, no overland, no sleeper**. NYE is
at the **Petronas Towers in KL**, not Bangkok. Home Mon 4 Jan on the CebPac
DMK→DVO flight. Everything below is a draft to review before it goes on the page.

**Why V3 is also the cleanest on paper:** because every leg is a flight, onward-travel
proof sorts itself out — the **SIN→KL ticket** proves we'll leave Singapore, the
**KL→BKK ticket** is our Thailand entry, and the **CebPac return** proves we'll leave
Thailand. No train bookings to chase, and the KL→BKK hop flies on **1 Jan (off-peak)**,
not the NYE-peak 31st, so it's cheaper too.

## Singapore — 3 nights (Dec 26–28)

### Day 1 · Sat 26 Dec — arrival, slow start
- Land Changi **00:25**. Immigration, then **Grab** to the hotel (~01:30 check-in). No
  MRT this late; five + luggage is two cars.
- Up around noon, no alarms.
- **Jewel Changi** (the Rain Vortex) — do it now, we arrived in the dark.
- **Gardens by the Bay** (Flower Dome + Cloud Forest), **Garden Rhapsody** light show at
  Supertree Grove.
- **Marina Bay** — Merlion, the skyline, Esplanade.
- Supper at **Lau Pa Sat** satay street.
- *Book the SG hotel for the night of Fri 25 Dec (we check in ~01:30 Sat). SGAC within 72h.*

### Day 2 · Sun 27 Dec — Universal Studios / Sentosa
- Rope-drop **Universal Studios**: Transformers, Battlestar Galactica, Revenge of the
  Mummy, Minion Land, Jurassic Park Rapids, WaterWorld.
- Dated Klook tickets, peak week. Sentosa extras (Palawan Beach, Skyline Luge) if time.

### Day 3 · Mon 28 Dec — the culture day *(the one we'd been dropping)*
- **Chinatown**: Heritage Centre, Sri Mariamman, Buddha Tooth Relic, **Maxwell Food
  Centre** (chicken rice, laksa).
- **Little India**: Sri Veeramakaliamman, Tekka Centre.
- **Kampong Glam**: Haji Lane, Arab Street, Masjid Sultan.
- Clarke Quay in the evening if anyone's up for it. Pack tonight, we fly tomorrow.

## Kuala Lumpur — 3 nights (Dec 29–31) · **New Year's Eve**

### Day 4 · Tue 29 Dec — fly SIN→KL, ease in
- Morning: **fly SIN→KL** (~1h05; Scoot/Jetstar/AirAsia/MAS, ~₱2,000–3,500). KLIA
  Ekspres to KL Sentral (~28 min), check in.
- Afternoon: **Merdeka Square** cluster (Sultan Abdul Samad, River of Life, I Love KL) —
  walkable end to end.
- Evening: **Jalan Alor** night food street. *MDAC within 3 days of entry.*

### Day 5 · Wed 30 Dec — KL full day
- Early: **Batu Caves** (rainbow stairs, Murugan statue) — before the heat.
- Midday: **Thean Hou Temple** (six-tier Chinese temple).
- Dusk: **Petronas Twin Towers** / KLCC Park — they light up after dark.
- Evening: **Bukit Bintang / Pavilion KL** (the Visit Malaysia 2026 Countdown Festival
  runs here 27–31 Dec).

### Day 6 · Thu 31 Dec — **NYE at the Petronas Towers**
- Daytime: keep it light. Bukit Bintang / Pavilion shopping, or rest for the night.
- Evening: **KLCC Park** — front-row to the **Petronas Towers fireworks**, "Rhythm of
  KLCC 2026" (live performances + fireworks, free street party). **Arrive hours early**,
  it's packed; bring a mat.
  - Guaranteed-view alternative: a rooftop countdown — **Traders Hotel SkyBar** (the
    classic Petronas-facing view), W KL, or Hyatt Centric — paid packages, book ahead.
- Getting back: LRT/MRT/Monorail run extended to **~3:00–3:30am** on 1 Jan.

## Bangkok — 3 nights (Jan 1–3)

### Day 7 · Fri 1 Jan — fly KL→BKK, ease in
- Late morning: **fly KL→BKK** (~2h, **off-peak** New Year's Day). Into **Don Mueang
  (DMK)** to match the return airport.
- Afternoon: check in, a light first evening after NYE — **ICONSIAM** riverside or a
  nearby night market. *TDAC within 72h of entry.*

### Day 8 · Sat 2 Jan — old town + the river
- **Wat Pho**, **Grand Palace & Wat Phra Kaew**, cross to **Wat Arun**, **Chao Phraya
  Express boat**, **ICONSIAM** or an optional dinner cruise.
- Grand Palace dress code (shoulders/knees); socks for temples; MRT Sanam Chai.

### Day 9 · Sun 3 Jan — market + last day
- **Chatuchak Weekend Market** (open Sundays), **Siam** shopping (MBK, Siam Paragon,
  CentralWorld, Erawan Shrine), **Yaowarat** (Chinatown) street-food dinner.
- Keep receipts for the airport VAT refund tomorrow.

### Day 10 · Mon 4 Jan — fly home
- Flight is at night, so a full last day: slow morning, last-minute Bangkok.
- ~18:00 to **Don Mueang (DMK)**. **21:15 CebPac DMK→DVO direct**, ~4h35, lands Davao
  in the early hours of Tue 5 Jan.
