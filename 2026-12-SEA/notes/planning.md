## Overland decision — 25 Jul 2026

The trip runs **by land from Singapore to Bangkok**. That constraint, plus NYE in
Bangkok on 31 Dec, fixes the middle of the itinerary almost completely:

- Malaysia → Thailand overland is a **two-day** run: KL→Padang Besar ETS ~5h30,
  then sleeper **#46**, the only daily service, departing Padang Besar 17:00 and
  reaching Bangkok 11:00 the next morning.
- Working backwards from NYE: sleeper on 30 Dec → KL day on 29 Dec → leave
  Singapore 28 Dec. Confirmed with the user.
- **Singapore gets two days** (26 Dec, 27 Dec Universal Studios), then departs the
  28th. User's call: two days is enough for the spots they want. Since the reroute
  the 26th starts at 09:50 rather than at a 01:30 check-in, and **Jewel Changi is
  now done on the way out of the airport** rather than as a separate trip back.
- **Kuala Lumpur gets one real day.** That is the cost of going overland.

**The single point of failure:** the sleeper runs once a day at 17:00. Miss it
and New Year's Eve in Bangkok is gone.

**Two ways to do 30 Dec — not yet decided (as of 25 Jul 2026):**

| | How long | Cost each | Arrives |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A · Overnight sleeper** | 16h 05m | ≈₱1,500, no hotel that night | 11:00 on the 31st |

once-daily train with a route that has 15 flights a day. Hat Yai airport is
45–55 min from the border station. Melaka was considered as a stopover and
**rejected — the user wants the full Kuala Lumpur day.**

## Risks

- **One-way ticket.** SG and TH immigration can ask for proof of onward travel,
  and airlines check it at the departure counter. Unresolved.
- **Peak season.** 25 Dec – 1 Jan is the most expensive week of the year across
  all three countries. Accommodation and the NYE night will not get cheaper.
- **Group of five.** Rooms for five are awkward — often two rooms or a
  serviced apartment. Affects the per-person maths.
