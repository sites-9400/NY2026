## Method note
- Browser: Playwright, one tab at a time (one stray Agoda "activities" tab opened by a mis-click and was closed). Closed the browser at the end. No bot wall on Booking.com or Agoda.
- Booking.com: searched "Kuala Lumpur City Centre KLCC", 28 Dec to 1 Jan, 5 adults, PHP, sorted by distance and loaded about 580 results out to 1.6 km. URL filters for apartments, free cancel, 7+ and 3 bedrooms were NOT applied by Booking (718 properties returned), so I filtered the cards by script (price, distance, "N bedrooms" text) and then opened the unit pages for the cheap candidates (7 pages) to read each bed list. Step-2 tax lines read for Best Apartment, City view, Homestay suite; Majestic City Center got no tax line (not needed: 2 reviews). Never typed or submitted.
- Booking card limits: the "bedrooms" text on a card is absent for many units, so units with no bedroom text and a pre-tax price above about 36,000 were assumed over cap without opening. Units under that price with no bedroom text were opened only if listed in the 1.4 to 1.7 km Times Square cluster (6 opened).
- Agoda: search results are virtualised; I scrolled each of the 98 near-Towers cards into view to read name, distance, price per night. Opened 12 property pages and read the room beds. Agoda prices on property pages are per night before tax and fees. The one checkout I opened (Vortex KLCC Apartments, Book and pay now) was read and left; nothing was typed or submitted. Agoda cashback and Insider price not applied. The profile is signed in to Agoda; I did not sign in or out.
- All-in: Booking = price + the "Excludes ... taxes and fees" line at step 2 (damage deposit excluded). Agoda = "Price" line on the first checkout page.
- Distances are the sites' own figures to the Petronas Twin Towers / KLCC. Kampung Baru flagged (Legasi 41).
- Re-confirmation: Legasi 41, Robertson, KL Elegant and both Majestic listings still appear on Booking at the same page prices today, but their step-2 totals were not re-read this run (Legasi's sign-in wall stands).
- Blocks: Klook 403. Booking sign-in wall (Legasi 41, earlier run, not retried). Agoda Vortex KLCC Apartments room list stopped loading after the checkout visit, so its free-cancel total is unread.
