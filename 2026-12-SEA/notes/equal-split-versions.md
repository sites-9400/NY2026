# Three equal-split versions — SG / KL / Bangkok only

Drafted 2026-08-15. The ask: divide the days as **equally** as possible across the
three countries, exploring **only Singapore, Kuala Lumpur and Bangkok** (no Pattaya,
no Johor Bahru stop, no Penang).

## The constraint that shapes all three

Two things are fixed and they fight a perfect split:

1. **We land in Singapore on Dec 26** (00:25, the booked one-way). Singapore is
   therefore always first, KL is always in the middle.
2. **The return is CebPac Don Mueang → Davao, Mon/Wed/Fri only.** After NYE that
   means **Mon 4 Jan** or Wed 6 Jan.

If **NYE stays in Bangkok (Dec 31)**, Bangkok has to own the whole tail of the
trip, so it ends up with ~4 days and the other two split the ~5 days before it.
A true **3/3/3 is only possible if NYE moves out of Bangkok.** That is the real
fork below.

All three versions **fly the two hops** (SIN→KL ~1h, KL→BKK ~2h). Flying is what
makes the days near-equal at all; the overland route spends two days in transit and
can't be balanced. Approx hop fares: SIN→KL ~₱2,000–3,500; KL→BKK base
~₱3,700–4,800, **but on 31 Dec it's NYE-peak ~₱6,000–10,000** (V1/V2 only — V3 flies
that hop on 1 Jan, off-peak).

## Version 1 — Singapore-leaning · NYE in Bangkok · home Mon 4 Jan
**Split: SG 3 / KL 2 / BKK 4**

| Dates | Where | Nights | The days |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dec 26–28 | **Singapore** | 3 | 26: land 00:25, slow start, Jewel + Gardens by the Bay + Marina Bay. 27: **Universal Studios**. 28: Chinatown, Little India, Haji Lane, Kampong Glam (the culture day) |
| Dec 29 | fly SIN→KL | — | ~1h, morning |
| Dec 29–30 | **Kuala Lumpur** | 2 | Batu Caves, Merdeka Square, Thean Hou, Petronas at dusk, Jalan Alor |
| Dec 31 | fly KL→BKK | — | ~2h, morning · **NYE-peak fare** |
| Dec 31–Jan 3 | **Bangkok** | 4 | 31: **NYE fireworks**. 1: rest / Siam shopping. 2: old town + river. 3: Chatuchak + Chinatown |
| Jan 4 | fly home | — | DMK→DVO 21:15 |

**Best for:** keeping NYE in Bangkok *and* getting Singapore's full culture day back
(the one we'd been dropping). KL is the thin one at 2 days.

## Version 3 — Truly equal 3/3/3 · NYE at Petronas (KL) · home Mon 4 Jan
**Split: SG 3 / KL 3 / BKK 3** — the only genuinely equal one

| Dates | Where | Nights | The days |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dec 26–28 | **Singapore** | 3 | 26: land 00:25, Jewel + Gardens + Marina Bay. 27: **Universal Studios**. 28: Chinatown, Little India, Haji Lane, Kampong Glam |
| Dec 29 | fly SIN→KL | — | ~1h, morning |
| Dec 29–31 | **Kuala Lumpur** | 3 | 29: Batu Caves + Merdeka. 30: Thean Hou, Bukit Bintang, Jalan Alor. 31: **NYE fireworks at the Petronas Twin Towers / KLCC** |
| Jan 1 | fly KL→BKK | — | ~2h, morning · **off-peak** (New Year's Day, not the 31st) |
| Jan 1–3 | **Bangkok** | 3 | 1: arrive, old town + river. 2: Chatuchak + Chinatown. 3: shopping + temples |
| Jan 4 | fly home | — | DMK→DVO 21:15 |

**Best for:** the actual equal split, and it's cheaper (the KL→BKK hop flies on 1 Jan,
off-peak, not the NYE-peak 31st). **The one real cost: NYE is in Kuala Lumpur at the
Petronas Towers, not Bangkok** — which reverses the trip's original anchor. KLCC does
a famous NYE countdown, so it's a strong NYE in its own right, but it's a different
trip in spirit. *(Verify KLCC NYE access/booking closer to the date.)*

## At a glance

| | SG | KL | BKK | NYE | KL→BKK fare | Home |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **V1** Singapore-lean | 3 | 2 | 4 | Bangkok | peak (31 Dec) | Mon 4 Jan |
| **V2** KL-lean | 2 | 3 | 4 | Bangkok | peak (31 Dec) | Mon 4 Jan |
| **V3** truly equal | 3 | 3 | 3 | **KL (Petronas)** | off-peak (1 Jan) | Mon 4 Jan |

**The decision underneath these:** do you want NYE in Bangkok (V1 or V2, and then just
choose whether SG or KL gets the extra day), or do you want a genuinely equal trip and
accept NYE at the Petronas Towers instead (V3)?

Everything here drops Pattaya and the overland border crossing. If either of those
still matters, it changes the maths again.
