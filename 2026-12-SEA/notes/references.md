## The one finding that should shape the plan

**Bangkok traffic dictates the itinerary, not the sights.** From Gary W's guide
(a Bangkok resident, 539 likes — the most endorsed guide for the city):

> "A short trip across town may take an hour, or more... Although it may go
> against your instincts, you are better off concentrating on one or two
> sections of town. Keep your transit times reasonable. You may not see
> everything you want, but you will enjoy your time more."

With five people and NYE crowds this matters more, not less. Pick two Bangkok
districts and stay in them, rather than a checklist scattered across the city.

## 🇸🇬 Singapore

**Guide:** [A local's touristy guide to Singapore](https://wanderlog.com/view/clgixuyaii)
· Szu Jin, lifelong local · Wanderlog "featured" · 19,102 views / 311 likes

Groups into four clusters, which map neatly onto half-days:

| Cluster | Places |
|---|---|
| **City centre** | Gardens by the Bay (Flower Dome + Cloud Forest, afternoon), Marina Bay + Merlion, Esplanade, **Lau Pa Sat** hawker centre for supper |
| **Cultural / hipster** | Haji Lane (indie cafés + boutiques), Masjid Sultan, Arab Street, National Museum, Chijmes, Clarke Quay for nightlife |
| **Multiculturalism** | Chinatown Heritage Centre, Sri Mariamman Temple (oldest Hindu temple), Masjid Jamae, Buddha Tooth Relic Temple, **Maxwell Food Centre** (chicken rice, laksa), Ann Siang Hill rooftop bars |
| **Sand + sea / shopping** | Sentosa Island; Orchard Road (ION Orchard) |

- **Jewel Changi** is at the airport — do it on arrival or departure, not as a
  separate trip. Relevant: we land Christmas Day.
- **Transport:** MRT and public bus are reliable and cheaper than Grab. For
  private hire, compare **Grab, Gojek and Zig** before booking — three apps,
  different prices.
- Tax refunds on Orchard Road shopping are claimed at the airport.

## 🇲🇾 Kuala Lumpur

**Guide:** [17 Best KL Attractions — First-Timer's Guide](https://wanderlog.com/view/dqhrylhlnx)
· Katherine Woo, repeat visitor · 1,642 views

| Cluster | Places |
|---|---|
| **Icons** | Petronas Twin Towers, Batu Caves (rainbow stairs), Menara KL, National Mosque, **Thean Hou Temple** — the author's personal favourite |
| **Colonial core** (all walkable together) | Merdeka Square, Sultan Abdul Samad Building, River of Life, I Love KL statue, KL Railway Station |
| **Neighbourhoods** | Petaling Street (Chinatown), Central Market, Brickfields (Little India), **Jalan Alor** night food street |
| **Green** | Perdana Botanical Gardens (free), KL Bird Park |

- **LRT/monorail plus short Grab rides** covers almost everything; the Merdeka
  Square cluster is walkable end to end.
- **Go before 11am** — beats both the heat and the crowds. Weekdays are quieter
  than weekends; our KL days would fall Tue–Wed, which helps.
- Honest notes from the author: Central Market was "boring", River of Life
  "sounds fancier than it is", Petaling Street similar to Singapore's Chinatown
  — worth knowing since we'll have just seen the real one.

**Note:** these guides are all Kuala Lumpur. **If we go to Johor Bahru instead**
(the cheap land hop from Singapore), none of this applies and it needs its own
research. That decision is still open.

## 🇹🇭 Bangkok

**Guide:** [Bangkok Guide](https://wanderlog.com/view/yyvzzjmclr) · Gary W,
lives in Bangkok · 25,774 views / **539 likes** — the strongest-endorsed guide
found for any of our three cities. 73 places.

Organised by district, which is exactly how the traffic problem forces you to plan:

| District | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Historic Quarter (Rattanakosin)** | Wat Pho and Wat Arun — the author rates both *above* the Grand Palace — plus the Grand Palace, Amulet Market. MRT Sanam Chai; Tha Thien + Tha Chang piers |
| **Khao San / Dusit** | Wat Saket (Golden Mount, city views), Wat Suthat + Giant Swing, Loha Prasat, Khao San Road itself ("worth a peek" even if not your scene) |
| **Chinatown** | Yaowarat Road — "one of the world's great foodie destinations", best explored with no agenda. Wat Traimit, Wat Mangkon, Sampheng Market, flower market. MRT Wat Mangkon |
| **Silom / Sathorn** | Lumphini Park, King Power Mahanakhon, Sri Maha Mariamman. Business district, lots of restaurants |
| **Siam Square** | MBK Center (great food court), centralwOrld (author's favourite mall), Siam Paragon, Erawan Shrine |
| **Sukhumvit** | Nightlife, Terminal 21, EmQuartier, Train Night Market Ratchada, Benchakitti Forest Park |

**Practical, straight from the guide:**
- **BTS + MRT + the Chao Phraya Express boat** can cover the city without ever
  taking a taxi. The river boat doubles as sightseeing.
- **Get a travel SIM at the airport** — first stop, before leaving arrivals.
- **Use the Grab app** rather than hailing. Taxi scams are the most frequent
  tourist problem.
- **Do not rent motorcycles.** The author is blunt about this.
- **Bring socks on temple days** — shoes come off, floors are hot.
- **Crossing the street:** never assume a driver will do what you expect. Make
  eye contact, hold a hand out at waist height, prefer crossing behind vehicles.
- Two airports — **Suvarnabhumi** (~20 mi out) and **Don Mueang** (~15 mi).
  Check which one our Malaysia flight lands at; it changes the transfer.
- A **Chao Phraya dinner cruise** is his top single recommendation for a
  first visit. Worth pricing for NYE — though see below.

**Gap: this guide says nothing about New Year's Eve.** It's a general city
guide. The fireworks night — where to watch, what books out, how bad transport
gets — still needs its own research. The riverside is where the guide points for
evening river views generally, but I haven't verified anything NYE-specific and
won't guess at it.
